WaveLens is a cross-platform marketplace for booking local surf photographers and videographers. I built the product end to end, including authentication, creator onboarding and approvals, editable profiles and portfolios, availability, booking requests, messaging, notifications, reporting, and admin moderation. The backend uses Supabase with PostgreSQL migrations, row-level security, storage, and an Edge Function, while Expo powers the responsive web, iOS, and Android experience.
